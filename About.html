About Us

Providing an individual learning stage and toolkit for every learner.

E-Careerpluz (established in 2010) is a Madurai-based training institute offering over 70 technical and non-technical courses. The institute focuses on job-oriented education and has successfully trained more than 36,000 students, with over 25,000 placements achieved.

They provide both online and in-person training across various fields such as mobile repairing, fashion designing, Tally, and software development. The institute emphasizes practical learning, skill development, and entrepreneurship, helping students build strong career paths.
